# Live demo checklist (about 5 minutes)

**Before the panel:** laptop on charger, internet on (three.js loads from the web), Chrome open, notifications off.
Start the museum with `Run_Museum.bat`, press F11 for full screen, and hard-refresh once with Ctrl+Shift+R.
Keep `../figures/` open as a fallback if the network fails.

1. **Gate** – the visit starts at the PDEU main gate; the museum is visible at the end of the drive. Walk in with W A S D and the mouse (Shift = faster).
2. **Lobby** – project signboards; point out that each placard shows that artifact's measured numbers.
3. **Close-up** – press **E** at a pedestal for the 360° view and its information panel; E or Esc to leave.
4. **Rotunda and galleries** – 30 main-set exhibits on the ground floor.
5. **Stairs and upper floor** – the 14 exhibits from the separate cut-off set.
6. Extras if asked: **V** first/third person, **C** change character, **M** map, `list.html` for the simple list.

Line to say: *a static website; 44 models of about 3 MB each; 60 fps on the laptop's integrated graphics.*
