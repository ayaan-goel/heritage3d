    About this museum

    Each object was reconstructed from rendered photographs only, using open-source photogrammetry (COLMAP structure-from-motion and OpenMVS dense reconstruction) on a laptop without a graphics card. Every exhibit uses the same capture budget: 92 photographs taken at 12° steps around the object. The resulting mesh was then reduced to about 100,000 triangles with a compressed texture so it loads quickly in a browser.

    The accuracy figure for each object compares the museum model with reference geometry decoded from the dataset's depth maps: F@0.002 R is the share of surface that agrees within 0.2% of the camera distance (1.0 = perfect).

    Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan.

    Source images: Cultural Heritage Synthetic Depth Dataset (CHSD), V. Arampatzakis, F. Arnaoutoglou, A. Koutsoudis & G. Pavlidis, 2024 — doi:10.5281/zenodo.11492154, rendered from models of the Virtual Hampson Museum.
