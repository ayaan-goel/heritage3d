heritage3d Virtual Museum

Cultural heritage artifacts digitized from ordinary photographs, reconstructed on a laptop CPU and optimized for the web. Start at the university's main gate, walk into the yellow arrow to enter, and step up to any exhibit for a 360° view. A second arrow by the museum's entrance doors leads back to the gate.
